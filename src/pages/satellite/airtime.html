Why satellite

Why Iridium for IoT.

Iridium's 66-satellite LEO constellation covers every square metre of Earth, including the poles. For NZ IoT deployments in alpine, offshore, or deeply rural sites, it's often the only option that works.

  • Global LEO coverage from 66 satellites in low Earth orbit (~781 km).
  • Pole-to-pole: works at any latitude, any longitude.
  • Compact terminals: tracker-sized devices to compact modems.
  • Low-latency for satellite: tens of seconds for SMS-class messaging.
  • Flexible plans: from per-message to monthly data buckets.
  • Resilient: not affected by terrestrial network outages, cyclones, or earthquakes.
