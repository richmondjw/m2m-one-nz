Starlink with Peplink

Supercharge your Starlink experience.

Starlink delivers excellent bandwidth from LEO. Peplink adds the rest: bonding multiple links (Starlink + cellular + fibre), automatic failover, traffic shaping, VPN, and a single pane of glass for fleet management at the edge.

Why combine

Why pair Starlink with Peplink.

Starlink on its own is single-link, with no built-in policy, no failover to cellular, no SD-WAN, and no central fleet management. Pair it with Peplink and it becomes enterprise-ready.

  • SpeedFusion bonding: combine Starlink with cellular for higher reliability and throughput.
  • Automatic failover: switch to cellular or fibre if Starlink degrades.
  • InControl 2: manage hundreds of edge devices from one console.
  • VPN to your data centre: site-to-site SD-WAN behind Starlink.
  • Traffic shaping & QoS: protect critical traffic at the edge.
  • Industrial-grade: ruggedised Peplink hardware for vehicles, vessels, and remote sites.
