Vehicle & Fleet

Cellular connectivity for telematics, tracking, and in-vehicle IoT.

Industrial-grade SIM cards and pooled data for GPS trackers, telematics units, in-vehicle Wi-Fi, dash cams, and connected vehicles across New Zealand. Multi-network failover, real-time location, central fleet management.

How it works

What this looks like with M2M One NZ.

Vehicles move, networks vary, and dead zones happen. M2M One NZ runs the connectivity behind a lot of NZ's vehicle and fleet IoT: GPS trackers, telematics black boxes, dash cams, in-vehicle Wi-Fi, and the connected vehicle programs of NZ vehicle OEMs. Industrial SIMs, pooled data, real-time location reporting, and the operational tooling to manage fleets of thousands of vehicles.

  • Mobile-first connectivity on Spark with AU partner roaming for trans-Tasman fleets.
  • Multi-network failover for vehicles crossing patchy coverage areas.
  • Real-time location reporting: low-latency uplinks, position fidelity.
  • Bulk SIM activation & APIs for fleet deployments and rolling rollouts.
  • Pooled data across the fleet, with per-vehicle alerts for outliers.
  • Industrial form factors: ruggedised SIMs and MFF2 for sealed telematics units.
