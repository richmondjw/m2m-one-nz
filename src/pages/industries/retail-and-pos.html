Retail & POS

Always-on EFTPOS and retail IoT.

Reliable cellular connectivity for EFTPOS terminals, self-checkouts, kiosks, vending, and in-store IoT across New Zealand. On Spark's nationwide network, with failover for stores where the wired connection goes down.

How it works

What this looks like with M2M One NZ.

M2M One NZ is the cellular layer behind a lot of NZ retail. We ship pre-provisioned industrial SIMs that drop into EFTPOS terminals, kiosks, and routers. They activate on first connection. They pool data across your store estate. They fail over to cellular when the ADSL or fibre at a store dies. They roam onto partner networks for trans-Tasman pop-up deployments.

  • EFTPOS-first: low-latency, PCI-aware connectivity for payment terminals.
  • Failover ready: cellular kicks in when wired internet drops, with seconds of switchover.
  • Pooled data across an entire store network: one bill, real-time visibility.
  • Quick to deploy: SIMs ship pre-provisioned, activate themselves.
  • Multi-form-factor: 2FF, Micro, Nano, MFF2, and M2M One eSIM for sealed kit.
  • 24/7 NZ-based support for store managers and IT teams.
Use Cases

Typical deployments.

EFTPOS terminals

Cellular-first or cellular-failover for payment terminals. Low data per transaction, high reliability.

Self-checkout kiosks

Always-on connectivity for self-serve and order-ahead kiosks. Pooled across the estate.

Digital menu boards

Cellular-backed digital menu boards in QSR. Survives the wired internet going down.

Vending & unattended

Telemetry from vending and unattended retail: stock, temp, sales sync.

In-store sensors & cameras

Footfall counters, fridge temp monitors, security cameras.

Pop-up stores & markets

Roaming SIMs for trans-Tasman pop-ups and seasonal sites.
