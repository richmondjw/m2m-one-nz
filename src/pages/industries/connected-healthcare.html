Cellular connectivity for medical devices and telehealth.

Industrial SIM cards and managed connectivity for connected medical devices, remote patient monitoring, telehealth carts, and clinical IoT across New Zealand. NZ Privacy Act 2020 aligned, on Spark's network.

How it works

What this looks like with M2M One NZ.

Healthcare IoT can't fail and can't leak. M2M One NZ supplies the cellular and managed connectivity that sits behind connected medical devices in NZ: from at-home patient monitors to in-clinic telehealth carts, with the privacy and uptime characteristics the sector expects.

  • NZ Privacy Act 2020 aligned operations with control plane in NZ.
  • Private APN & VPN options for sensitive clinical data.
  • Pooled data across a patient cohort or device fleet.
  • Industrial-grade SIMs: ruggedised form factors for medical devices.
  • Always-on resilience: managed failover for critical equipment.
  • NZ-based support: Auckland team, no offshore call centre.
