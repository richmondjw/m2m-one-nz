Smart Agriculture

Cellular and satellite IoT for farms and rural sites.

Industrial-grade connectivity for water tank monitors, soil sensors, irrigation controllers, livestock tracking, and farm fleet across rural New Zealand. Spark coverage on the cellular layer, Iridium satellite where cellular doesn't reach.

How it works

What this looks like with M2M One NZ.

Rural NZ is where IoT connectivity is hardest. Cellular coverage thins out. Sites are remote. Devices run on solar and need to be low-power. M2M One NZ runs the connectivity layer behind a lot of NZ smart-ag deployments: Spark cellular where coverage is good, LTE-M and NB-IoT for low-power sensors, and Iridium satellite for the last 1% of the country.

  • Spark rural coverage across NZ, with LTE-M and NB-IoT for low-power sensor classes.
  • Iridium satellite for sites where cellular doesn't reach, on the same bill.
  • Pooled data plans: from 0.5 MB for telemetry, up to GBs for image-based monitoring.
  • Bulk activation & APIs for deploying hundreds of sensors in a season.
  • Long-life SIMs: industrial-grade for outdoor enclosures and harsh environments.
  • Resilient: managed failover and multi-network options on perimeter properties.
Use Cases

Typical deployments.

Water & tank monitoring

Level sensors for water tanks, troughs, irrigation reservoirs. Low data, high reliability.

Soil & weather sensors

In-paddock soil moisture, temperature, frost monitoring on LTE-M / NB-IoT.

Irrigation control

Centre-pivot, drip, and pump control with two-way cellular.

Livestock tracking

GPS collars and ear-tag trackers with cellular and satellite blend.

Farm fleet & assets

Tractor, ute, and asset tracking across the property.

Cool stores & produce

Temperature monitoring for cool stores, packing, and cold-chain logistics.
