Digital Signage

Cellular connectivity for digital signage at scale.

Industrial SIM cards and pooled data for digital out-of-home (DOOH), in-store screens, transport signage, and kiosks across New Zealand. Spark coverage, central billing, no surprises.

How it works

What this looks like with M2M One NZ.

Digital signage runs on networks. When those networks are flaky, the screens go black, and the network operator gets a phone call. M2M One NZ supplies the cellular layer that keeps the screens online: industrial-grade SIMs with pooled data, real-time monitoring, and the operational hygiene to keep a fleet of hundreds or thousands of screens humming.

  • Pooled data across the screen network: a fleet of 200 screens shares one bill and one data pool.
  • Industrial form factors: 2FF, Micro, Nano, MFF2 for sealed signage enclosures.
  • Static IP & VPN options for content delivery from a private CMS.
  • Real-time alerts: data spikes, offline screens, signal degradation.
  • Multi-network failover in regions with patchy coverage.
  • Bulk activation & APIs for fast deployments and rolling rollouts.
Use Cases

Typical deployments.

Outdoor billboards

DOOH screens at intersections, shopping precincts, transit stops. Always-on, weatherproof, with cellular as primary or failover.

In-store digital menus

QSR menu boards, retail point-of-decision displays. Pooled data across store estate.

Transport signage

Bus stop displays, station signage, real-time passenger info.

Wayfinding kiosks

Mall and venue wayfinding kiosks. Touch interaction with low data per session.

Corporate signage

Office lobby displays, conference room signage.

Event & pop-up

Temporary deployments where running cable isn't feasible.
