3G Shutdown

What NZ's 3G shutdown means for your IoT.

Spark is shutting down its 3G network in New Zealand to refarm spectrum for 4G and 5G. For most IoT deployments, that's a 1-line change. For some, it's a real migration project. Here's where M2M One fits in.

The basics

What changes, what doesn't.

If your IoT devices are 4G LTE, 5G, LTE-M, or NB-IoT-capable, the 3G shutdown doesn't affect you. If they're 3G-only or 2G fallback to 3G, you need a plan.

  • 4G LTE / 5G devices are unaffected.
  • LTE-M / NB-IoT devices are unaffected.
  • 3G-only devices need replacement before the shutdown.
  • 2G/3G fallback: 2G already shut down, so 3G fallback ones go offline at 3G shutdown.
  • Legacy alarm panels: many use 3G; replacement programs are underway.
  • Legacy POS terminals: replace with 4G models.
