Partner Programs

Grow your business with M2M One NZ.

From device makers integrating cellular into their products, to systems integrators deploying our SIMs at scale, M2M One NZ partners help bring industrial IoT to market. Talk to us about partnership programs.

Programs

Partner types.

OEM partners

Embed M2M One eSIMs (MFF2) and connectivity into your hardware products.

Systems integrators

Deploy M2M One SIMs and devices across customer fleets, with project support.

Resellers

Resell M2M One connectivity to your customer base under your relationship.

Technology partners

Hardware vendors (routers, sensors, modems) integrated with M2M One.

Channel programs

Volume tiering, joint go-to-market, and co-funded initiatives.
