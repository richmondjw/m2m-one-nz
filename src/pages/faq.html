FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about SIM cards, data plans, satellite, support, and the trial. If you don't see your question, talk to us.

What network do M2M One NZ SIMs run on?

Spark's nationwide network. We're a wholesale partner of Spark NZ, with access to 4G, 5G, LTE-M, and NB-IoT across the country.

What form factors do you offer?

Standard (2FF), Micro (3FF), Nano (4FF), Industrial-grade, Machine Solderable (MFF2), and M2M One eSIM. Same network, same plans, different physical packages for different deployments.

How small or large can the data plan be?

From 0.5 MB per month for low-bandwidth telemetry, up to 1 TB for video and bulk data. Plans can be pooled across your fleet or sized per SIM.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. We'll send up to 3 SIMs and a starter plan, no lock-in. Request a trial here.

Do you offer satellite connectivity?

Yes, through Iridium and Blue Sky Network, billed on the same invoice as your IoT SIMs. Useful for offshore, alpine, and other sites where cellular doesn't reach. See satellite plans.

What hardware works with M2M One NZ SIMs?

Most industrial IoT hardware. We pre-test routers and modules from Peplink, Sierra Wireless, Robustel, and others through our sister company M2M Connectivity. See all devices.

How do I activate SIMs?

SIMs ship pre-provisioned. Activation is automatic on first connection, or you can stage activation via our Control Centre and API for large fleets.

Where is your support team?

Auckland, New Zealand. 24/7 in-house. Phone, email, and Control Centre tickets. No outsourced call centres, no chatbot triage.

What about data security and privacy?

Carrier-grade IPX backbone, ISO/IEC 27001-aligned operations, NZ Privacy Act 2020 compliant. Optional private APN and VPN for sensitive deployments. Our control plane stays in New Zealand.

Can I get AU coverage too?

Yes. M2M One NZ SIMs roam on partner networks in Australia. For AU-only deployments, we can also coordinate with M2M One Pty Ltd (our Semtech sister company in Australia).
