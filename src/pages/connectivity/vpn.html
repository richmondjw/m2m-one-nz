VPN Services

Private connectivity for sensitive IoT.

When your IoT traffic shouldn't touch the public internet, our VPN services keep it on a private path from device to data centre. Private APN, static IP, and site-to-site IPsec, on Spark's NZ network, with NZ-based operations.

Options

What we offer.

Private APN

A private Access Point Name where your SIMs land. Traffic routes via a private path, never touches the public internet.

Static IP

Static IP allocation per SIM for inbound connectivity, monitoring, and remote access.

Site-to-site IPsec

IPsec tunnel from M2M One's carrier-grade IPX to your data centre or cloud.

Cloud direct connect

Direct path to AWS, Azure, GCP without traversing the public internet.

MPLS bridge

Bridge cellular traffic into your existing MPLS WAN.

Compliance-ready

Operations aligned with NZ Privacy Act 2020 and ISO/IEC 27001. Suitable for healthcare, financial, and critical infrastructure.
