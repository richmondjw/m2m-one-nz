IoT Networks

The networks behind M2M One NZ.

Industrial IoT runs on a stack of networks. Spark NZ for the cellular layer. Partner carriers for AU roaming. Iridium and Blue Sky for satellite. Private APN and VPN for sensitive deployments. Here's how they fit together.

Stack

Network layers.

Spark NZ (cellular)

Spark's nationwide network: 4G LTE, 5G, LTE-M, NB-IoT. Our sole NZ carrier.

AU partner roaming

Trans-Tasman roaming on partner networks in Australia.

Iridium satellite

Global LEO satellite, 66 satellites in low Earth orbit.

Blue Sky Network

Enterprise satellite tracking and communications.

Private APN

Custom APN where your SIMs land, away from the public internet.

Site-to-site VPN

IPsec / SD-WAN between M2M One's IPX and your data centre.
