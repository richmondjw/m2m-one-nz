Site survey

What we look at on a site survey.

We can't map coverage on every floor of every building from a postcode lookup. For sites that matter, we do a real survey.

  • RF measurements at the planned device location, not just on the street.
  • Radio technology mix: which of 4G/5G/LTE-M/NB-IoT actually performs there.
  • Antenna recommendations: indoor, outdoor, MIMO, directional options.
  • Failover strategy if cellular is marginal: satellite, multi-modem, fixed backup.
  • Building penetration: signal characteristics inside steel structures, basements, etc.
  • Interference assessment: nearby base stations, industrial RF noise.
