IoT SIM Pricing

Plans for every size of deployment.

Pricing should be predictable. M2M One's plans are sized from 0.5 MB per month (telemetry) up to 1 TB (video). Pool across SIMs or fix per-SIM. Monthly or annual. No lock-in.

Plan Tiers

Plan tiers (indicative).

Telemetry (sub-MB)

0.5 MB - 5 MB / SIM / month. For tank monitors, alarm panels, low-frequency sensors.

Low-data IoT

5 MB - 50 MB / SIM / month. EFTPOS, vending, low-data IoT.

Standard IoT

50 MB - 500 MB / SIM / month. Kiosks, signage with light updates, GPS trackers.

Higher-data IoT

500 MB - 5 GB / SIM / month. Connected vehicles, richer telematics, signage with video.

Video / mobile broadband

5 GB - 50 GB / SIM / month. Security cameras, in-vehicle Wi-Fi, telehealth carts.

Heavy data

50 GB - 1 TB / SIM / month. Video surveillance, mobile broadband, passenger Wi-Fi at scale.
