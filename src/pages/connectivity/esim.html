M2M One eSIM

Embedded SIM (MFF2) for sealed deployments.

When your hardware can't have a removable SIM tray, M2M One eSIM (MFF2 form factor) is soldered directly to your PCB at manufacturing. Same Spark NZ network, same data plans, same Control Centre, but no physical SIM card to lose, replace, or tamper with.

Built for OEMs

Why eSIM (MFF2).

If you're an OEM integrating cellular into a product, MFF2 (Machine Form Factor 2) is the cleanest path. It's a soldered chip, not a card.

  • No removable SIM tray: simpler enclosure, lower BOM, no service ports.
  • Tamper-resistant: SIM can't be removed or swapped in the field.
  • Vibration and shock proof: no contact reliability issues.
  • Smaller footprint: 6mm x 5mm, suitable for wearable and small-form factors.
  • Extended temperature: -40°C to +105°C operating range.
  • Same network & plans as standard M2M One SIMs.
