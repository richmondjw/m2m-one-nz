Built for industrial use

Why our SIMs.

Consumer SIMs aren't built for fleets, sites, or unattended devices. Our SIMs are.

  • Industrial-grade construction: extended temperature and vibration tolerance for ruggedised deployments.
  • Pre-provisioned: no manual setup, no APN typing, no first-boot delays.
  • Pooled billing: one invoice per fleet, with per-SIM visibility.
  • Real-time alerts: data spikes, offline SIMs, signal degradation, all surfaced.
  • Static IP & private APN options for sites that need them.
  • No lock-in: month-to-month, cancel any time, pause SIMs when not in use.
