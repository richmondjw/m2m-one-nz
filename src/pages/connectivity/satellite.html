Satellite

Reliable coverage. Even where mobile can't reach.

Whether you're operating in alpine NZ, offshore, on the water, or in the last 1% of rural country, M2M One's Satellite Connectivity Plans keep your IoT devices online where traditional networks fall short. Iridium and Blue Sky Network, billed on the same invoice as your IoT SIMs.

Satellite features

What you get on satellite.

Satellite isn't cheap, but for the sites where it's the only option, it's essential. We make it as simple as cellular: one bill, one support team, one place to manage everything.

  • Iridium global LEO: 66 satellites in low Earth orbit (~781 km), pole-to-pole coverage.
  • Blue Sky Network: enterprise satellite for aviation, marine, and remote terrestrial.
  • Works with existing hardware: standard Iridium-compatible modems, terminals, and antennas.
  • Flexible data plans: from per-message pricing up to monthly data buckets.
  • Transparent pricing: no hidden per-byte surcharges.
  • One bill with your IoT SIMs & data plans. One support team in Auckland.
  • Full New Zealand support: NZ-based team for setup, monitoring, escalation.
