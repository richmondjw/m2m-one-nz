Data Plans

From 0.5 MB to 1 TB. Pooled or per-SIM.

Choose data plans sized to your devices. Telemetry sensors run on under a megabyte a month. Video deployments need gigabytes. We have plans for both, and everything in between, pooled across your fleet or fixed per SIM.

How it works

Plan structure.

Per-SIM plans

Each SIM has its own data allowance. Simple, predictable. Best for small fleets and single-device deployments.

Pooled plans

A shared data pool across many SIMs. Hot SIMs draw more, quiet SIMs draw less, the fleet averages out. Best for variable usage.

Monthly or annual

Month-to-month for flexibility, or annual commit for better rates on larger fleets.

0.5 MB to 1 TB

From sub-MB telemetry plans to TB-scale plans for video, image-based monitoring, in-vehicle Wi-Fi.

Alerts & cap rules

Set per-SIM and per-pool alerts at 50/80/100% of allowance. Hard caps or soft-throttle on overage.

AU roaming

Plans extend onto partner networks in Australia for trans-Tasman fleets.

Sizing guide

What runs on what.

If you're trying to figure out which plan size fits your deployment, here's a rough guide. Talk to us for precise sizing on your specific traffic shape.

  • < 5 MB / month: tank monitors, alarm panels, low-frequency telemetry sensors.
  • 5-50 MB: EFTPOS terminals, vending, low-data IoT.
  • 50-500 MB: kiosks, digital signage with light content updates, GPS trackers.
  • 500 MB - 5 GB: connected vehicles, telematics with rich data, signage with video updates.
  • 5-50 GB: security cameras, in-vehicle Wi-Fi, telehealth carts.
  • 50 GB - 1 TB: video surveillance fleets, mobile broadband sites, passenger Wi-Fi at scale.
