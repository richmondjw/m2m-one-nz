About Us

Industrial IoT for New Zealand. Built locally. Backed globally.

M2M One NZ Ltd is a New Zealand industrial IoT specialist. We design, integrate, and operate cellular and satellite connectivity for fleets, sites, and devices across Aotearoa, on Spark's nationwide network. We've been doing it for over a decade, and we're part of Semtech, the global leader in connectivity silicon and platforms.

Our work

What we do.

We're the connectivity layer underneath a lot of New Zealand's industrial IoT, from EFTPOS terminals and digital signage to fleet trackers and remote sensors. We handle the SIM cards, the data plans, the carrier integration, and the control plane so our customers can focus on the device and the business.

  • SIM cards & data plans on Spark's nationwide NZ network, with 4G, 5G, LTE-M, and NB-IoT.
  • Satellite airtime through Iridium and Blue Sky Network for sites mobile networks can't reach.
  • Managed services: IPX backbone, VPN, failover, real-time monitoring, and central billing.
  • Hardware through M2M Connectivity (our sister company): Peplink, Sierra Wireless, Robustel, and more.
  • Local support: 24/7 NZ-based team out of our Auckland Airport office.
