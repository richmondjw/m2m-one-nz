LPWAN

Low-power wide-area for battery-friendly IoT.

LPWAN technologies (LoRaWAN, LTE-M, NB-IoT) make multi-year battery life on IoT sensors possible. Combined with Spark's national LTE-M and NB-IoT footprint, LPWAN deployments scale to tens of thousands of sensors across NZ.

Variants

Technology variants.

LoRaWAN

Unlicensed sub-GHz mesh. Suits private networks, campus deployments, and where cellular doesn't reach.

LTE-M (Cat-M1)

Spark nationwide. Best mix of battery life and bandwidth for most LPWAN cases in NZ.

NB-IoT

Spark nationwide. Deepest penetration, longest battery life. Best for utility metering and environmental sensors.

Sigfox

Available in NZ via partners. Ultra-narrow band, very low data rates, low cost.

Mioty & Wi-SUN

Specialty LPWAN technologies for utility and industrial applications.

Hybrid deployments

LPWAN in the field, cellular at the gateway. Common pattern for farm and infrastructure.
