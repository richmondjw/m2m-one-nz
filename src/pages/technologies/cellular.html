Cellular

Cellular IoT on Spark's NZ network.

Spark NZ operates one of the world's most advanced cellular networks for IoT, with full national coverage of 4G LTE, expanding 5G, plus LTE-M and NB-IoT for low-power and low-bandwidth IoT classes. M2M One is a Spark wholesale partner for industrial IoT.

Technologies

Cellular radio technologies.

4G LTE

Workhorse of NZ IoT. National Spark coverage. Suits most data-heavy IoT classes (POS, telematics, signage, cameras).

5G

Expanding Spark 5G coverage in NZ. Useful for high-bandwidth IoT (in-vehicle Wi-Fi, video surveillance, immersive applications).

LTE-M (Cat-M1)

Low-power, narrow-band variant of LTE. Excellent battery life. Suits trackers, low-data sensors.

NB-IoT

Even lower power, lower throughput than LTE-M. Deep building penetration. Suits utility metering, environmental sensors.

Cat-1 / Cat-4

Medium-bandwidth, lower-cost modems for telemetry. Common in legacy IoT.

Cat-NB1 / NB2

NB-IoT classes for ultra-low-power IoT with multi-year battery life.

Sizing guide

Choosing the right cellular class.

For most NZ IoT, 4G LTE is the default. The cases where LTE-M or NB-IoT win are battery-powered devices, narrow telemetry, or deep-indoor deployments. 5G wins for richer data.

  • POS & payments: 4G LTE (low latency for transactions).
  • Vehicle telematics & fleet: 4G LTE; 5G where bandwidth helps.
  • Digital signage: 4G LTE or 5G if content updates are heavy.
  • Smart agriculture sensors: LTE-M or NB-IoT for battery life.
  • Smart metering: NB-IoT for deep-indoor penetration.
  • Security cameras: 4G LTE or 5G for video bandwidth.
