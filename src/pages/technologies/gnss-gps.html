GNSS & GPS

Global positioning for connected things.

Knowing where a device is matters. GNSS (Global Navigation Satellite System) gives any IoT device a position from satellites. GPS is the US system; modern receivers combine GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, and BeiDou for redundancy and accuracy.

Variants

Technology variants.

GPS (USA)

The original GNSS. Universally supported, well-understood, sub-5-metre accuracy.

GLONASS (Russia)

Russian GNSS. Combined with GPS for redundancy and northern-hemisphere accuracy.

Galileo (EU)

European GNSS. Combined with GPS/GLONASS, gives sub-3-metre accuracy.

BeiDou (China)

Chinese GNSS. Asia-Pacific accuracy advantage, increasingly globally supported.

Multi-GNSS receivers

Modern modules use all 4 for accuracy and resilience.

RTK / DGPS

Differential GPS reaches centimetre-grade accuracy. Useful in surveying, agriculture, autonomy.
