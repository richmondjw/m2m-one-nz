Short Range

Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and sub-GHz for the last metre.

Short-range technologies handle the ‘last metre’ of IoT — from a sensor to a gateway, from a phone to a tracker, between devices in a vehicle. M2M One supplies Bluetooth-based sensors (ELA Innovation), in-vehicle Wi-Fi, and short-range mesh.

Variants

Technology variants.

Bluetooth LE

Ubiquitous in phones, low power, suits ELA Innovation sensors and personal-area networks.

Wi-Fi 6 / 6E

In-vehicle, in-vessel, on-site Wi-Fi. High throughput, established ecosystem.

Sub-GHz mesh

Long-range, low-power mesh for industrial deployments (smart agriculture, utilities).

Zigbee & Thread

Mesh networking for smart-building IoT, low power per node.

BLE Beacons

Position and proximity beacons for in-store, in-warehouse, on-site.

UWB (Ultra-Wideband)

Centimetre-grade positioning for asset tracking indoors.
