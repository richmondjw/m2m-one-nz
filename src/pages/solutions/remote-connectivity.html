Remote Connectivity

For the sites that aren't on the map.

Sites past the edge of fibre, past the edge of nationwide cellular, or both. M2M One's blend of Spark cellular (LTE-M and NB-IoT for the rural reach), satellite (Iridium and Blue Sky), and SD-WAN routers gets you connected where conventional ISPs can't help.

Toolkit

Tools for remote.

Spark rural coverage

Spark NZ has the broadest national cellular footprint, particularly LTE-M / NB-IoT.

LTE-M / NB-IoT

Lower-power, deeper-penetration radio classes for marginal coverage.

External antennas

Yagi, MIMO, panel, and high-gain antennas to extend coverage at the device.

Iridium satellite

Global LEO for sites with no cellular at all.

Blue Sky Network

Enterprise satellite for fleets and marine/aviation applications.

Solar + low-power

Industrial SIMs in solar-powered, off-grid enclosures.

Use Cases

Where remote connectivity wins.

Remote IoT is harder, more expensive, and slower to design than urban IoT. The wins are bigger too — these sites usually couldn't be monitored or controlled at all before.

  • Smart agriculture: tank monitors, soil sensors, fence monitors on rural Spark.
  • Conservation: bird tracking, pest monitoring, environmental sensors.
  • Mining & resources: remote-site SCADA, plant telematics.
  • Marine: vessel tracking, hull monitoring, crew safety.
  • Alpine & high country: ski-field IoT, conservation, infrastructure monitoring.
  • Disaster recovery: rapid connectivity restoration after major outages.
