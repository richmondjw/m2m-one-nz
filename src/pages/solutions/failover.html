Backup & Failover

For sites that can't go down.

When the primary WAN fails (fibre cut, modem dies, ISP outage), cellular kicks in. M2M One's industrial SIMs paired with multi-WAN routers from Peplink, Sierra Wireless, or Robustel give you sub-second failover, with policy controls for what traffic switches and what waits.

Architecture

How failover is built.

Failover isn't a single product, it's an architecture. The right pattern depends on the criticality of the site and the cost of downtime.

  • Hot standby: cellular link is always active; traffic flips on failure of primary.
  • Cold standby: cellular link comes up only when primary fails (slower, cheaper).
  • Active/active: both links carry traffic; failover is implicit (no flip).
  • Policy-based routing: critical apps go cellular even when primary is up.
  • Multi-cellular: two cellular carriers (Spark + roaming) for full diversity.
  • Satellite as last resort: for the most critical sites, satellite behind cellular.
