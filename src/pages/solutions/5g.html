What's different

What 5G unlocks for IoT.

5G isn't just ‘faster 4G’. It changes which IoT use cases are practical at scale.

  • Bandwidth: gigabit-class throughput where it's available.
  • Latency: low-latency air interface, important for real-time control.
  • Density: more devices per cell, useful at scale.
  • Slicing: dedicated network slices for critical applications (over time).
  • Roaming: 5G plans roam onto partner networks for trans-Tasman fleets.
  • Backward compatible: 5G modems fall back to 4G LTE where 5G isn't yet rolled out.
Use Cases

Where 5G fits in NZ IoT today.

Connected vehicle programs

5G in fleet for richer telematics, in-vehicle Wi-Fi, and OEM connected-car services.

Video surveillance fleets

5G uplink for IP cameras with high resolution or many concurrent streams.

Mobile broadband

5G as primary or failover for remote / vehicle-based bandwidth.

Industrial automation

5G for low-latency industrial control where Wi-Fi 6 isn't viable.

Pop-up sites

5G for festival, event, and emergency connectivity.
