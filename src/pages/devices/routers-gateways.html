Routers & Gateways

Industrial cellular routers for fleet & site.

Field-proven cellular routers and gateways from Peplink, Sierra Wireless, and Robustel. Pre-configured for M2M One NZ SIMs, with the firmware, VPN, and failover policies typical of NZ deployments.

What you get

What we ship pre-configured.

Routers can take a day to provision properly. We do that work upfront so the device arrives ready.

  • M2M One SIM pre-inserted and pre-activated.
  • APN, VPN, and firewall policies configured to your deployment.
  • Firmware up to date at the time of shipping.
  • Remote management registered (InControl 2, Sierra ALMS, RCMS, etc).
  • Failover rules configured if the deployment uses multi-WAN.
  • NZ-based support for the lifecycle of the device.
