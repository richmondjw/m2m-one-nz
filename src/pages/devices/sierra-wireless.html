Sierra Wireless

Industrial cellular routers, gateways & modules. Now part of Semtech.

Sierra Wireless (now Semtech) makes some of the most widely-deployed industrial cellular hardware in NZ: AirLink routers, RV/AC-series modules for OEM integration, and the AirLink Management Service for fleet visibility. M2M One pre-configures Sierra Wireless devices with our SIMs.
